About Us

HackWithIndia is India's largest and most impactful hackathon community. With a mission to foster innovation, collaboration, and learning, HackWithIndia has become the hub for tech-enthusiasts, developers and creative problem-solvers across the country.

In just the first 10 months, HackWithIndia organised over 20 high-energy hackathons, solidifying its role as a driving force in India's rapidly growing tech ecosystem.

What We Do

🚀

Organize Hackathons

We organise full-scale hackathons and challenges that bring together coders, designers, innovators and tech-curious minds to solve real-world problems.

🎓

Mentorship & Workshops

We provide mentorship, workshops and networking opportunities so that participants don't just compete — they grow.

🌐

Build Community

We build and nurture a strong community of developers across India (and beyond) working on technologies like Web3, AI, IoT etc.

Who Can Join
